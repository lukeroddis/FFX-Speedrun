Privacy and legal

What this site stores, the other services it uses, and who is responsible for the content it shows.

This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan site: a personal project for my own Final Fantasy X speedrunning, which anyone is welcome to use. It has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics.

Made with AI

I built this site with help from Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. Claude wrote much of the code and some of the text, working from my directions, and I review what goes in. Notes, skips and route contributions are my own, with credit given for video content.

What the site stores

The site sets no cookies and doesn't track you. It does remember a few things in your browser's local storage, so the site works the way you left it:

  • your settings: light or dark theme, the notes' route choices, game text language, what to show, and the Skips layout;
  • where you are in the notes, and the Power Sphere count;
  • your work in the Route Builder, Map Editor and Sphere Grid Editor, including any reference image you load.

All of it stays on your device and is never sent to this site or anyone else. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

Reporting a problem

If something on the site, or something it links to or shows, is inappropriate, wrong, or yours and you'd like it taken down, please open an issue on GitHub. Reported videos and links are reviewed and removed where appropriate.
