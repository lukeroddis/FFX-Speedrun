## What it does

In the second half of play, the match is paused so Tidus can be substituted in for Wakka around the 3 minute mark.

Scoring a goal in this ~2 second window means the game registers the goal, but does not play the short cutscene for displaying the scoreboard, nor does a Blitzoff occur where the opposing team receive the ball.

In this second half, when you have the ball with Letty/Jassu, swim to the bottom of the field. Around the 2:20 mark, advance towards the opposing team goal, and try to lure Abus away. Tidus will swim ahead of Abus, so you can pass to Tidus.

Try to avoid getting caught by other opponents, and perform Sphere Shot around the 2:50 mark.

If the timing is correct, the game will register a goal as the screen fades to black, skipping the subsequent cutscene and Blitzoff.

It is also possible to do this at the end of each half when the timer hits 5:00, but this is far more difficult and is near-frame perfect.
