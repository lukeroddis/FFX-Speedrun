## What it does

On PC, FMVs can be skipped. The FMV Storage Skip transfers that skip from one FMV to another, which saves time when the later FMV is longer.

## In Any%

In Zanarkand, on the second FMV where the Sinscales fly out of the Sinspawn, don't skip. Instead, press **Start** towards the end of the FMV. This lets you skip the one after Tanker.
