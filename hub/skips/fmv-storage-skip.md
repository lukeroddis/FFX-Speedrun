## What it does

On PC, some FMVs, but not others, can be skipped. The FMV Storage Skip is a method that transfers a skip from one FMV to another, which saves time when the later FMV is longer.

## In Any%

In Zanarkand, on the second FMV where the Sinscales fly out of the Sinspawn, don't skip. Instead, press **Start** towards the end of the FMV. This lets you skip the one after Tanker as you leave Zanarkand.

In Kilika, during the sending FMV, skip this but also immediately press Start after skipping (so Start -> Square -> Start). If fast enough, the option to skip will appear for the Luca arrival FMV, which is not normally skippable.
