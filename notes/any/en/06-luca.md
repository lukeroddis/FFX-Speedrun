:::blitzball
After each Blitzoff...
* *If Graav wins the ball:*
  * Immediately press Square to change Formation to Mark Mode, then switch back to Normal Formation before Letty reaches Graav. 
  * Letty will either swim past or attempt to tackle Graav and fail. Graav swims towards your goal and be intercepted by up to three Aurochs, who will likely win the ball. When you win the ball...
* Immediately press Square and change to Manual A to gain freedom to move around, and Normal Formation.
* Swim to your own goal, and pass to Tidus. Tidus needs to pass once to level up for the second half, so swim close to Jassu (bottom right Auroch) and pass to him.
* If you're not being followed, you can swim to your goal or the bottom of the map and usually not get intercepted. 
* Else, you can perform the **Jassu train** and never be caught by any player as long as you keep swimming and avoid getting too close to the Goers. 
* Do this until the timer reaches 5:00 signifying the end of the first half.

* SD, Tidus should level up. Make no changes to the team and proceed to the second half.

* Repeat the above Graav method if you lose the Blitzoff.
* When you have the ball, swim to goal to avoid the Goers. When they swim back to their half, swim south to the bottom of the map and wait.
* Around the 2:20 mark, start swimming towards their goal, lure Abus away from Tidus and pass when Tidus is in an open clearing.
* Around the 2:50 mark, shoot with Sphere Shot near their goal. When timed correctly, this occurs as the screen fades to black, leading to the **Blitzoff Skip**.

* SD during Wakka protest. Third 'half':

* Repeat the above Graav method if you lose the Blitzoff.
* If winning, hide behind goal or Jassu train.
* If you still need to score, try the same scoring method described above but with Wakka.
* If Overtime, select the team again and try the above method to score.

Winning is faster due to obtaining a Strength Sphere, but you can proceed with a loss.
:::
