# Zanarkand

party: Tidus

1. Press Select to skip Cutscene (about 15 seconds in on PS2)
2. Talk to the three kids, name Tidus, then speak to the women, walk down the center
3. Hold Up+Right running down the road. SD and run up past the crowd. Skippable FMV (2:30)
4. Down to Auron, SD, Skippable FMV x2 (2:30), SD
:::if version=pc
5. FMV Storage Skip: On the second FMV where the Sinscales fly out of sinspawn, don't skip, press **Start** towards the end of the FMV. This lets you skip the one after Tanker.
:::
