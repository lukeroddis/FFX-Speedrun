# Kilika

1. SD upon exiting the boat, go up and left, SD.
:::if version=pc
2. Skippable FMV (2:00). FMV Storage Skip: Press Start immediately after skip for option to skip to appear for next FMV.
:::
3. SD. Exit inn, go right to Wakka, SD. Go left and up to Kilika Woods, SD.

:::battle Lancet Tutorial
party: Tidus, Kimahri, Lulu
SD
* Kimahri: Lancet
* Switch Kimahri for Wakka
* Wakka: Defend
* Tidus: Attack
* *If Valefor died on Sin Fin:*
  * Switch Lulu for Yuna
  * Yuna: Summon Valefor
  * Valefor: Boost x2
  * Valefor: Fire
* *Else:*
  * Lulu: Fire
:::

:::map Kilika Woods
file: kilika-woods
:::

4. Following the path on the map shown, go left and up and open the chest for the Scout weapon.

:::spheregrid
* *If Tidus has 3 levels:*
  * Move ← ← or ↖
  * Flee, Agil +1 (and Str +1 if you didn't get it already)
route: kilika-tidus
:::

:::equip
* Wakka: Scout / Ice Ball
* Wakka: Any Armguard (optional)
* Tidus: Ice Brand (if you received one)
:::

5. **Formation:** Tidus, Wakka, Lulu

6. Continue up the hidden path, following the map. Fight encounters as described below.
7. Need 45 AP on Tidus, which is 5 kills (Overkills count as 2). This is your main source of Speed Spheres but you can obtain the rest later.
8. You can benefit from kills beyond the first 5 but do not intentionally farm encounters and do not go beyond 17 kills.
9. You can get the Luck Sphere on the return journey out of Kilika Woods.

:::encounters
* *If only Ragoras:*
  * Tidus: Flee
* Dinonix: Tidus: Attack
* Killer Bee: Wakka: Attack
* Killer Bee: Lulu: Blizzard
* Yellow Element: Lulu: Water
:::

10. After exiting the woods, SD.
11. **Formation:** Tidus, Yuna, Lulu
12. **Touch the Save Sphere**

:::battle Sinspawn Geneaux | 3,000HP
* *If Tidus is going before Yuna:*
  * Tidus: Defend
* *Else:*
  * Switch Yuna for Wakka
  * Wakka: Defend
  * Tidus: Defend
  * Switch Lulu for Yuna
* Yuna: Summon Valefor
* Valefor: Energy Ray
* Valefor: Fire x3
* Valefor: Energy Ray
Guaranteed 4 Power Spheres, additional 2 Power Spheres if Rare Drop.
:::

13. SD on stone steps and temple. Go into the temple, walk up to Wakka and choose to Pray.
14. SD inside temple and go up the steps. Wait for the lift, walk up to the door and SD.

:::trial
* Take the sphere from the pedestal
* Place into the door and remove it
* Place sphere into the next door and remove it
* Place the sphere into the right holder
* Touch glyph
* Take the sphere from the next room
* Place it into the left holder in the previous room
* Take the glyph sphere from the pedestal
* Place it in the Fire Room
* Take the sphere that you put into the right holder
* Use it to open the door at the end of the Fire Room and remove it
* Enter the Fayth room
:::

15. In Fayth room, SD and speak to Wakka first. Try to leave room, SD and name Ifrit.
16. Run down to exit temple, CS (0:40), SD.
17. **Formation:** Tidus, Wakka, Lulu
18. Run back through Kilika Woods the same way you entered, treating encounters as before.
19. Get the Luck Sphere as shown in the map if you haven't done so already.
20. Go down and right to S.S. Winno. SD.
21. On S.S. Winno, CS (1:10), exit door on the right, SD with Oaka. Run outside, go up to the top deck for a Wakka and Lulu cutscene, SD.
22. Run up to the blitzball at the front of the boat. CS (1:10).
23. Follow the tutorial but fail the minigame. Do **not** get Jecht Shot.
24. Run up to Yuna, SD. Do not save. FMV (0:30).
:::if version=pc
25. Skippable FMV (0:30) if you successfully buffered the FMV skip at the beginning of Kilika.
:::
