# Baaj Temple

party: Tidus

1. Hold O and swim Down+Left to talk to Jecht. SD when Tidus wakes up. 
2. Swim around the rock and north to the temple.
3. CS, hold O and swim Down+Right, CS.

:::battle Sahagins and Geosgaeno
* Tidus: Attack the Sahagins
* CS (0:30)
* Tidus: Defend against Geosgaeno until CS
:::

4. Heal Tidus with Potions. Open Config and switch Cursor to Memory, and Aeons to Short.
5. CS, go down and left through the door. Pickup Flint and exit.
6. Go north and through the entrance. Climb steps and pick up the Withered Bouquet. Return to the fire in the center of the room. CS (2:10)

:::battle Klikk | 1,500HP
* Tidus: Attack x6, Potion once if Tidus has less than 227HP
* CS, SD
party: Tidus, Rikku
* Rikku: Use Grenade x1
* Tidus: Attack
* Rikku: Successful Steal x2
* *If Tidus has less than 114 HP:*
  * Rikku: Potion Tidus
* Tidus: Attack for the rest of the fight
* Rikku: Attack for the rest of the fight
:::

party: Tidus, Rikku

7. CS (2:30). Talk to Rikku for tutorial, SD.
8. Hold O and swim Down+Left. On next screen hold O and swim forward.

:::encounters
- Piranhas:
    - Rikku: Steal Grenades
    - Tidus: Attack
Monitor for bonus Power Spheres or Speed Spheres
:::

9. Swim Down+Right to **Touch the Save Sphere**, swim forward, then right across the station.

:::encounters
- Piranhas:
    - Rikku: Steal Grenades until you have 6, Attack after
    - Tidus: Attack
Monitor for bonus Power Spheres or Speed Spheres
:::

10. CS, Swim to center, then return to main area. Heal Rikku with Potions if she is below 250HP.

:::battle Tros | 2,200HP
* *If fewer than 6 Grenades*
  * Rikku: Steal

* Rikku: Use Grenade x6
* Tidus: Attack x2, Standby otherwise
Guaranteed 2 Power Spheres, Overkill gives +2 Power Spheres
:::

11. Swim up to the next screen. CS, and follow red arrow to CS (0:50)
12. SD. until Tidus gets food. CS (3:00). Walk to Rikku. CS (2:30), SD during Al Bhed dialogue. Don't save.
